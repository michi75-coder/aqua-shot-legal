Terms of use and privacy

Contact us

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to us at appyapp.contact@gmail.com. If you can, include your phone model and the AquaShot version (at the bottom of the settings).

How is my daily goal worked out?

If you entered your weight: about 33 ml per kilogram, adjustable in the settings ("My daily goal"). On top of that comes the water the alcohol calls for: 10 ml of water per gram of alcohol (volume × ABV ÷ 100 × 0.8). These are estimates for guidance, not medical advice.

Why don't coffee or soda make up for alcohol?

They count toward your baseline, as they hydrate almost like water. But to make up for alcohol, AquaShot only counts water: a soda would mostly add sugar.

How do I add the widget?

  • Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  • Tap "Edit", then "Add Widget" (or the "+" at the top).
  • Search for "AquaShot", pick a size and tap "Add Widget".

A glass of water added from the widget is saved in the app the next time you open it.

I don't get reminders

Check that they are on in AquaShot's settings and that notifications are allowed for AquaShot in the phone's Settings. A reminder is only sent if your gauge stays in the red for more than 45 minutes.

My data

Everything stays on your phone: no account, no server. "Reset the gauge" clears the current day; deleting the app deletes all your data. See the privacy policy.

Does AquaShot measure my blood alcohol?

No, never. AquaShot only tracks your hydration and never tells you whether you can drive. When in doubt, don't drive.

Alcohol abuse is dangerous for your health. Please drink responsibly.
